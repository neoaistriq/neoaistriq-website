About NeoAistriq

Built to Cut the Fluff.
Wired for Results.

NeoAistriq is an AI-native lean services firm and Agentic AI Lab based in Frisco, TX. We exist to make outcomes-based AI delivery accessible to SMB owners and executives who don't have enterprise budgets — but have enterprise ambitions.

Our Story

Why We Built This

NeoAistriq was founded on a conviction that became impossible to ignore: the technology was ready, but the delivery model was broken.

After years in technology — watching engagements bloat with unnecessary layers of process, headcount, and deliverables that looked impressive but didn't move a single business metric — the question became unavoidable: what would a services firm look like if you designed it from scratch for outcomes, not optics?

The answer is NeoAistriq. A lean, AI-native practice where every engagement starts with a business metric and ends when that metric moves. Where AI agents do the work that used to require teams. Where fractional cost doesn't mean fractional results.

What We Believe

Our Principles

01

Outcomes over outputs

A delivered document isn't a result. A moved metric is. We design every engagement around the result, not the deliverable.

02

Lean is a feature, not a constraint

Two-person teams move faster, communicate better, and stay accountable in ways large teams structurally cannot. We're lean on purpose.

03

AI should compound

Every framework we build, every agent we deploy, every engagement we run makes the next one faster and better. Our model gets stronger over time.

04

Honest over agreeable

If we don't think AI is the right answer for your problem, we'll tell you. We'd rather lose an engagement than fail a client.

Our Network

Part of a Larger Ecosystem

NeoAistriq is the AI-native services and lab arm of Fractal KX, a Frisco, TX-based venture studio. We operate alongside a network of operators, partners, and builders across India, Southeast Asia, and the United States.

We maintain commercial relationships with select technology partners and stay actively engaged with the frontier AI research and tooling community. Our independence from any single vendor or platform is intentional — it keeps our recommendations honest. NeoAistriq also serves as the AI-native operating entity for the Fractal KX venture ecosystem, applying the same lean, outcomes-based model we bring to clients across our internal ventures.
