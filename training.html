Why This Training Is Different

Most AI Training Teaches Tools.
We Teach Outcomes.

The internet is full of courses on how to use ChatGPT, how to write prompts, and how to automate tasks. That's useful — but it's not what moves a business.

What SMB leaders actually need: how to identify where AI will generate measurable ROI, how to evaluate AI initiatives before committing budget, how to govern AI deployments without a dedicated IT team, and how to hold vendors accountable. That's what AI for Business Outcomes teaches. No coding. No technical prerequisites.

What You'll Learn

A Framework Your Business Can Use the Next Day.

01

The Outcomes-First Mindset

Why most AI projects fail to deliver business value — and the mental model that separates successful AI adopters from everyone else. You'll learn to evaluate any AI opportunity through one lens: what metric does this move, and by how much?

02

AI Opportunity Mapping

A structured method for identifying the 3–5 highest-ROI AI opportunities in your business. You'll apply this framework to your own operation during the session and leave with a prioritized opportunity map.

03

Evaluating and Scoping AI Initiatives

How to evaluate vendors, scope engagements, and set success criteria before signing anything. What questions to ask. What red flags to watch for. What good looks like.

04

Governing AI in a Lean Organization

How to manage AI deployments without a dedicated IT team — data governance basics, risk management for SMBs, and how to build internal accountability for AI outcomes.

05

Building Your AI Roadmap

Bringing it together: a 90-day AI roadmap for your business, prioritized by impact and effort, with a clear first step and a defined success metric for each initiative.
