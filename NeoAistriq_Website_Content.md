## SECTION: Our Delivery Framework

**Headline:**
Four Phases. One Outcome.

**Phase 1 — Define**
We start every engagement by getting precise about the problem. What's the business metric? What's the current state? What does success look like in 30, 60, 90 days? This phase takes days, not weeks — and it shapes everything that follows.

**Phase 2 — Design**
We architect the agentic solution — mapping the workflow, selecting the tooling stack, and designing the human-in-the-loop checkpoints where they matter. You review and approve before we build anything.

**Phase 3 — Build & Deploy**
We build fast, using our Lab's existing accelerators and frameworks where possible. Iteration is built into the process — you see working software early, not at the end.

**Phase 4 — Measure & Optimize**
After deployment, we measure against the success metric defined in Phase 1. We optimize, document, and hand off. For Managed AI Operations clients, this phase becomes an ongoing loop.
